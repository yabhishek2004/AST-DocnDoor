Our Journey

The Evolution of DocnDoor

2015 - The Spark

Founded by a team of healthcare professionals who recognized the disconnect between hospital care and home recovery. Started with a simple idea: what if medical care and home support worked as one unified system?

2017 - First Integration

Launched our first integrated care program, combining post-surgical nursing with household management. The results were transformative - faster recovery times and significantly reduced stress for families.

2019 - Technology Leap

Introduced our proprietary care coordination platform, enabling real-time communication between medical teams, home specialists, and families. This innovation set a new industry standard.

2022 - National Expansion

Expanded operations across multiple cities, building a network of certified professionals and establishing partnerships with leading healthcare institutions and home service organizations.

2025 - Innovation Leader

Today, we're pioneering AI-assisted care planning, predictive health monitoring, and sustainable service models that make premium care accessible while maintaining our core values of compassion and excellence.
