Deep Dive

Still Curious? Expand A Topic

How do medical and house-care schedules stay coordinated?

One concierge owns your calendar. Nursing visits always get priority and house-care resets are layered around recovery windows. We share the live itinerary with your whole family.

Can you work with my existing physician or clinic?

Yes. We set up secure tele-rounds or simply follow written orders. Most hospitals appreciate our daily vitals reports and photos, which we send through encrypted links.

What about products for allergies or newborns?

Our house-care captains stock unscented, pediatric-safe, and green products. If you have a favorite brand we’ll catalog it and train your pod on exactly how to use it.

Do you travel outside city centers?

We cover the greater metro area plus select suburbs. When we travel farther, we build a custom mileage add-on so crews are fairly paid and you know the cost upfront.
