Common Questions

Click on any question to reveal the answer.

What types of medical care services does DocnDoor provide?

DocnDoor offers a comprehensive range of medical care services including in-home nursing support, medication management, wound care, vital signs monitoring, physical therapy, post-surgical care, and coordination with your primary care physician. Our licensed clinicians are trained to handle various medical conditions and can provide hospital-level care in the comfort of your home.

Are your medical staff licensed and certified?

Yes, absolutely. All our medical staff are fully licensed, certified, and background-checked. Our team includes registered nurses (RNs), licensed practical nurses (LPNs), physical therapists, and other healthcare professionals who meet state licensing requirements. We regularly verify credentials and provide ongoing training to ensure the highest standards of care.

What house care services are included in your offerings?

Our house care services include deep cleaning, regular maintenance cleaning, laundry services, organization and decluttering, meal preparation, grocery shopping, pet care, and specialized cleaning for post-medical situations. We use eco-friendly products and can customize our services to meet your specific needs and preferences.

How far in advance do I need to schedule a visit?

For routine visits, we recommend scheduling at least 24-48 hours in advance to ensure availability. However, we understand that medical needs can be urgent, so we offer same-day scheduling for emergency medical situations. For house care services, same-day or next-day appointments are often available depending on your location and service requirements.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards, debit cards, and digital payment methods. We also work with insurance providers for eligible medical care services. Payment is typically processed after service completion, and you'll receive a detailed invoice. For recurring services, we offer convenient auto-pay options.

Can I combine medical care and house care services in one visit?

Yes! One of DocnDoor's unique advantages is our ability to coordinate both medical and house care services. Our care coordinators can schedule a medical professional and house care specialist to visit on the same day, or even coordinate their efforts for a seamless experience. This integrated approach ensures comprehensive care while maximizing convenience.

Do you provide services for seniors and individuals with chronic conditions?

Absolutely. We specialize in providing care for seniors and individuals managing chronic conditions. Our team is experienced in managing conditions such as diabetes, heart disease, mobility issues, and post-surgical recovery. We work closely with families and primary care physicians to create comprehensive care plans tailored to each individual's needs.

Are your cleaning products safe for homes with children and pets?

Yes, we prioritize safety and use eco-friendly, non-toxic cleaning products that are safe for children and pets. All our house care specialists are trained in safe cleaning practices and can accommodate specific product preferences or allergies. We're happy to discuss your concerns and customize our cleaning approach to meet your family's needs.

What is your cancellation policy?

We understand that plans can change. You can cancel or reschedule appointments up to 24 hours in advance at no charge. Cancellations made less than 24 hours before the scheduled visit may incur a fee, though we're flexible for emergencies and medical situations. For recurring services, you can modify or pause your schedule at any time through your client portal.

How do I get started with DocnDoor services?

Getting started is easy! Simply contact our concierge team via phone, email, or our online contact form. We'll schedule a consultation to understand your needs, discuss service options, and create a customized care plan. Once you approve the plan, we'll match you with the appropriate specialists and schedule your first visit. The entire process typically takes just a few days.
